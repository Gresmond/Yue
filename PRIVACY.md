# Yue privacy policy

Yue is an app for learning to read and write Mandarin. It has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics of its own.

## On your device

Your learning progress is stored on your device. If you turn on Google sync, your Google account's email address and sign-in token are stored there too.

## Google sync

Google sync is optional. If you turn it on, Yue asks Google for these permissions:

- **Your email address and account identifier**, to show which account is signed in and to keep each account's progress separate.
- **Yue's own hidden app folder in your Google Drive (`drive.appdata`)**, to store your learning progress so it can be shared between your devices. Yue can't see any of your other Drive files.

This data goes directly between your device and Google. It isn't sent to the developer or anyone else, and it isn't sold or used for advertising or to train AI. Google handles it under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Yue's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

- **Signing out** in Yue removes the sign-in from that device. Your progress stays on the device and in Drive.
- **To delete what's in Drive**, open [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com), go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Yue, and choose **Delete hidden app data**.
- **To remove Yue's access**, go to your [Google Account's third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
